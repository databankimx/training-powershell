# 2. The Terminal

Chapter 1 got your terminal ready. This chapter teaches you to use it. You'll learn to read the prompt, move from folder to folder, and tell the computer where a file lives, which is the skill that every later chapter depends on.

If the terminal has ever felt like a locked room with a light on in the corner, this chapter is the one that turns the light on. Nothing in it is advanced. It's just unfamiliar, and familiar things look different after you've practiced them a few times.

## What you'll do

1. **Read the prompt.** Find out where you are, and what the line of text in front of the cursor is telling you.
2. **Paths, absolute and relative.** Learn the two ways to name a place, and when each one is the right choice.
3. **Moving around.** Use `cd`, `ls`, the shortcuts that save typing, and the history of commands you've already run.
4. **Summary.** Put it together, and try the quizzes.

## A word before you start

Every example in this chapter is a command you can type yourself. Most of them only change where you are, so you can't damage anything by trying them. The exceptions are marked, and the exercises use the practice folder, which you already know.

Open a PowerShell 7 window before you begin. If you're not sure it's the right one, check with `$PSVersionTable.PSVersion.Major`, as you did in Chapter 1. It should say 7.
