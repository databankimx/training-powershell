# 3. Commands and Help

So far you've used commands without asking much about them. You've typed `Get-ChildItem`, `Set-Content`, and `Remove-Item` and taken them on trust. This chapter makes that trust earn its keep. You'll learn how a command is built, how to ask PowerShell what one does, and how to find the right command when you don't know its name.

Nobody memorizes every command, and nobody should try. The people who are fast with PowerShell aren't the ones who know the most names. They're the ones who know how to ask.

## What you'll do

1. **What a command is.** The verb and noun pattern, parameters, and switches.
2. **Getting help.** `Get-Help`, its examples, and the parts of a help page that matter most.
3. **Finding a command.** `Get-Command`, searching by verb and noun, and a first look at `Get-Member`.
4. **Summary.** Put the chapter together, and try the quizzes.

## Before you start

The exercises here don't use the practice folder's files, so you won't need any setup. Open PowerShell 7, and check the version as you did in Chapter 1 if you're not sure.

Help text changes a little between versions of PowerShell, and it can differ on your computer from what the examples show. The shape is the same, and the lessons point out the parts you can rely on.
